## 1. Real derby car paint: visual style, themes, numbers, sponsors, roof signs, family involvement

### Takeaway
Real derby cars are cheap, loud, hand-done paint jobs. Spray paint is the main tool, and the look is graffiti-like or folk art rather than polished. Every car carries big contrasting numbers (doors and roof) and usually a contrasting driver's door. Themes run from in-jokes and slogans ("GIT ER DONE") to cartoon and pop-culture murals (SpongeBob, Star Wars, Venom), movie themes ("Mean Girls"), school buses, and even student social-issue art. Families, kids and school classes often paint the cars, and this is a known crowd draw.

### Cited Findings
**Overall look**
- Derby cars "are repainted, often in loud, garish designs and on low budgets"; spray paint is often used to mark names, slogans and identification. — [Wikipedia: Demolition derby](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demo_derby)
- A blogger says spray-painted numbers on derby cars "look like graffiti" and add "to the mystique of this subculture sport." — [Patterns and Tones blog, "Demolition Derby Rock and Roll"](https://patternsandtones.blogspot.com/2013/07/demolition-derby-rock-and-roll.html)
- Builders advise bright colors so drivers can see each other. Bright paint plus the driver's number on the driver's door helps others avoid ramming it, which is against the rules. — search summary of derby guides incl. ### Inferences
- Art direction: "folk art under a stadium light." Use uneven spray edges, overspray halos, drips, hand-lettered names, brush-stroke murals over rust and dents, and mismatched panels. Numbers should be the boldest element, legible from the stands and from above.
- Theme presets with proven appeal: cartoon/pop-culture murals (shark rider, SpongeBob-style, superheroes), school bus, girly/pink "stand out" cars, redneck slogans, family tributes, class/school art projects, local business names, and message cars.
- Family and kid painting is a real social ritual. A co-op or "let your kid paint the car" mode (simple stamps, finger-paint brush) fits the subject.
- The contrasting driver's door is both a style signature and a safety rule. It is a good fixed element of the template.

## 2. Paint techniques, materials and rules

### Takeaway
Rattle-can spray paint is the confirmed default. Rulebooks set the hard constraints: numbers at least about 18 in. tall in contrasting color on both front doors and the roof, a mandated driver's-door color at many events (white, red or yellow), a roof sign at some events (at least 12x12 in.) and a ban on offensive or vulgar words and pictures. I could not source detailed technique guides (house paint and rollers, stencils, masking).

### Cited Findings
- Spray paint is frequently used to mark names, slogans and IDs. — [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demo_derby)
- Numbers: at least 18" tall in clear contrasting paint. — [Moses Lake Roundup 2024 rules](https://www.rodeoticket.com/rodeos/moses-lake-roundup/2024/register)
- Numbers at least 18" high and 16" long, in a bright contrast color, on each front door and the roof. Driver's door must be white. — [Miles City 2017 Derby Rules](https://milescitychamber.com/mcLuci/uploads/2017/06/2017-Derby-Rules.pdf)
- Driver's door must be red (full-size class). — [Alaska State Fair 2026 rules](https://www.alaskastatefair.org/site/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-Alaska-State-Fair-Demolition-Derby-Rules-Full-Size-Large-Cars.pdf)
- Driver's door must be yellow. — [Cumberland Fair rules](https://cumberlandfair.com/wp-content/uploads/2022-derby-rules-02.pdf)
- Driver's door must be a contrasting color. — [Carlton County Fair (MN) rules](https://carltoncountyfairmn.com/demolition-derby-rules)
- A roof sign of at least 12x12" is required. Offensive or vulgar words or pictures are prohibited anywhere on the vehicle. — [Pine County Fair Derby Rules Packet 2024](https://www.pinecountyfair.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/DerbyRulesPacket.pdf)
- Roof sign is recommended. If there is no roof sign, numbers must go on the roof and both sides. — [Moses Lake Roundup 2024](https://www.rodeoticket.com/rodeos/moses-lake-roundup/2024/register)
- Officials may remove signage they consider in bad taste. — [Maitland Showground (AU) Derby Rules](https://maitlandshowground.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Maitland-Derby-Rules.pdf)
- Spray-paint behavior (from pinewood-derby sources, adjacent hobby only): rattle cans dry fast enough for 2+ coats a day, mixing paint types causes crackling, and a rushed last coat leaves a bumpy finish. — [Scout Life](https://scoutlife.org/hobbies-projects/projects/11508/paint-a-pinewood-derby-racer/); [DerbyTalk](https://derbytalk.com/viewtopic.php?t=6366) (not about full-size derby cars)

### Inferences
- Why numbers: judges and flaggers must identify who is still active, who made a hit, and who sandbagged. The roof number serves high vantage points (announcer, grandstand). This is the likely reason for roof plus door placement, though no source states it in judge-scoring terms.
- Game rules to copy: required number slots (both doors and roof, plus an optional roof-sign mesh), a minimum number size, a forced contrast check against the base color, a colored driver's door per event, and a word/image filter that mirrors the real "no offensive" rule.

### Gaps
- No citable source on house paint with rollers or brushes, stencils, masking tape, primer use, or flat vs gloss for full-size derby cars. The bigheavyworld.com "How To Paint A Demolition Derby Car" guide would cover this but could not be loaded.
- No source explained in its own words why numbers are needed for scoring.

## 4. How games handle paint, decals, numbers, sharing, damage and spray mechanics

### Takeaway
There are two dominant models. (a) Vector vinyl layer editors (Forza, GT7, NFS): players stack primitive shapes and decals into layers per car zone, save reusable vinyl groups, and share through an in-game storefront with human moderation. (b) Texture skins (BeamNG, Trackmania, FlatOut, Rocket League mods): painted against a UV template outside the game. Wreckfest 2's "CRAP-IT" (Nov 2025) is the closest genre precedent: it treats rust, dents, paint removal, dirt and decals as cosmetic choices visible in multiplayer. Free-draw spray exists mostly in sims (My Summer Car: per-part color only). Graffiti games use gesture or input sequences instead of freehand drawing.

### Cited Findings
**Forza (Motorsport / Horizon)**
- A single vinyl or decal is one layer. Higher-numbered layers draw over lower ones. FH3 per-zone caps: front and rear bumpers 1,000 each, left and right sides 3,000 each, top/hood 3,000, wing 1,000. — [Forza Horizon 3 Game Guide/FAQ (forum)](https://forums.forza.net/t/forza-horizon-3-game-guide-faq/36955/51)
- FM4: side and top panels 1,000 layers; front and back 500. — [GTPlanet FM4 Livery Editor Tips](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/forza-motorsport-4-livery-editor-tips-and-tricks.275894/)
- Players build complex shapes by stacking and masking primitives, which burns through layers. They have requested SVG upload and "open source" vinyl group sharing. — [Forza forums: SVG request](https://forums.forza.net/t/allow-uploading-custom-decal-vinyl-in-vector-format-svg/811485?page=2); [Open Source groups request](https://forums.forza.net/t/setting-to-share-vinyl-groups-as-open-source/628506)
- Some primitive shapes have caused share failures (bug report). — [Forza forums](https://forums.forza.net/t/sharing-liveries-for-the-racing-truck-fails-probably-because-of-the-square-and-half-disc-shapes/36217)
- FH6 can import liveries and vinyls only from FH5, and only ones that were shared publicly. — [Forza Support](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/50300032354451-How-to-Transfer-Pre-Existing-Vinyls-Decals-to-Forza-Horizon-6)
- FH5 save cap: 500 files for tunes and paints (players complained, since the game launched with 534 cars). — [Forza forums](https://forums.forza.net/t/increase-file-limit-500-limit-for-a-game-that-launched-with-500-cars-fh5/553394)

**Gran Turismo 7**
- Custom decals are uploaded as SVG files (max 15 KB) through the "Decal Uploader" on gran-turismo.com, then shared to Showcase. GT7 can import liveries and decals from GT Sport. — [RacingGames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/article/how-to-upload-decals-to-gran-turismo-7); [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/gt7-import-sport-liveries-20220202/); [GT7 manual: Livery Editor](https://gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/manual/liveryeditor/01)

**Need for Speed**

**Wreckfest / Wreckfest 2 (closest genre match)**
- Content Update 4 (Nov 2025) added "CRAP-IT." Bugbear describes it as adding rust, damaged paint, dirt and decals, all visible to everyone in multiplayer. Gameplay-affecting mods are promised for later. — [OnlineRaceDriver](https://www.onlineracedriver.com/2025/11/04/wreckfest-2-content-update-4-adds-visual-customisation/); [THQ Nordic](https://thqnordic.com/node/1155)

- Skins are painted on a UV template ("skin_UVs" PNG). UV map 1 is non-mirrored and meant for paint schemes. **Trackmania / ManiaPlanet**
- In-game "paint a car" editor. Shininess is driven by the diffuse texture's grayscale alpha (DXT5 with mips). Users report custom skins sometimes don't appear, and one saw an all-black car after adding decals. — [Maniaplanet forum t=31789](https://forum.maniaplanet.com/viewtopic.php?t=31789); [Maniaplanet docs: import a car skin](https://doc.maniaplanet.com/customization/importer/import-a-car-skin); [Forum t=42379](https://forum.maniaplanet.com/viewtopic.php?t=42379)

**Rocket League**
- Official system: preset decals plus primary/accent colors and paint finishes in the garage. Custom image decals exist only through the PC-only BakkesMod/AlphaConsole mod, with community UV templates. — [Kaizen RL Gumroad](https://kaizenrl.gumroad.com/l/custom-paint); [RL starter pack](https://kaizenrl.gumroad.com/l/rlstarterpack) (third-party sources)

**FlatOut**
- FlatOut's damage is physics-based deformation. I found no source describing paint scraping. — [Wikipedia: FlatOut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FlatOut_(video_game))

**Spray-can / freehand mechanics**
- My Summer Car: spray cans (13 colors, 29 mk each at Teimo's shop) paint body panels and parts; one can covers 10 parts. Paint does not remove rust and leaves dull marks over it. Use: hold the can and press F. Mods add gradients, matte, carbon and a paint booth. — [My Summer Car Wiki: Spray can](https://my-summer-car.fandom.com/wiki/Spray_can); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/516750/discussions/2/282992646984156536/); [Nexus: Advanced Paint Studio](https://www.nexusmods.com/mysummercar/mods/10230)
- Bomb Rush Cyberfunk: graffiti is sprayed at fixed spots with one button. Each design is a stick-direction sequence (e.g., "Up, left, right, down"; bigger pieces need longer diagonal sequences), viewable in the phone menu. — [BRC Wiki: Graffiti](https://bombrushcyberfunk.wiki.gg/wiki/Graffiti); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bomb_Rush_Cyberfunk)

### Inferences
- For a derby game, a hybrid fits the subject: (1) a small vector layer editor (shapes, fonts, number templates, stickers) for legible numbers and sponsor names; (2) a freehand "rattle can" brush that paints into a texture for the hand-done look, with overspray, drips and limited colors; (3) a CRAP-IT-style wear layer. Layer caps (Forza-style per-zone budgets) keep sharing and network sync cheap.
- A BRC-style gesture "tag" (stick sequence stamps a pre-made piece) could be a fast pre-heat mini-game for controller players who will not freehand.
- Damage interplay matters more here than in racing games: liveries are meant to be wrecked. A post-match "before/after" photo of the trashed livery is a natural shareable moment.

## 5. Technical approaches and moderation

### Takeaway
Standard building blocks: (a) render-to-texture painting into the car's UV space (needs a clean, non-overlapping UV set, as BeamNG's "UV map 1" shows); (b) projected decals (URP/HDRP Decal Projector), which struggle on meshes that deform at runtime, a key problem for derby crumple; (c) layered shading where a damage mask (often vertex color) reveals clear coat cracks, then primer, then bare metal, with dirt or mud on top. Moderation of user liveries needs report flows and human review. Forza's FH6 anime-livery controversy shows the risks of unclear rules.

### Cited Findings
**Painting and decals**
- GPU runtime mesh-painting assets paint directly onto 3D meshes across PBR channels (albedo, normal, metallic, roughness, height). — [Simple Painter](https://deepwave.itch.io/simple-painter-unity); [TexturePaint](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/painting/texturepaint-5927)
- Simplest method: a render texture used as a mask. Runtime virtual-texture masks require all painted objects to share a UV space. — [Unity discussion](https://discussions.unity.com/t/shader-that-erases-part-of-a-texture-to-show-another-texture-based-on-a-decal/463832); [Unreal forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/car-wash-paint-walls-in-realtime/751564)
- URP Decal Projector projects Shader Graph decal materials. The mask map packs AO (R), smoothness (G), emission (B) and alpha. — [Unity URP Decal Shader Graph docs](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.universal@15.0/manual/decal-shader.html); [HDRP Decal Shader](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/hdrp/manual/Decal-Shader.html)
- Mesh-baked decal system caveat: it "will not work nicely with skinned mesh renderers or other meshes that get distorted at runtime." — [Driven Decals docs](https://github.com/Anatta336/driven-decals/blob/master/Documentation~/DrivenDecals.md)

**Damage and paint layers**
- softbodydriver (open source): vertex color serves as a damage mask, re-uploaded only when damage changes. The car_paint shader dulls and cracks the clear coat, flakes paint to primer, adds scratches, exposes bare steel at the worst spots and darkens creases. Dents under about 3% crush leave paint intact. — [GitHub PR #8](https://github.com/dibs999/softbodydriver/pull/8)
- Proposal: vertex colors reveal a scratch texture, with channels selecting scratch direction (custom shader needed). — [Randomation Vehicle Physics issue #9](https://github.com/JustInvoke/Randomation-Vehicle-Physics/issues/9)
- Real paint stack: primer, then basecoat, then clear coat. Primer shows gray or white when exposed, metal shows bright silver, and bumpers show black plastic. — [Wikipedia: Automotive paint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automotive_paint); [Drive Detailed](https://drivedetailed.com/how-to-identify-the-type-of-scratches-on-cars-paint/)
- BeamNG: damaged material variants must also be reskinned (deformGroups take priority). — [BeamNG docs](https://documentation.beamng.com/modding/materials/vehicle/skinsystem/)

**Moderation**
- Forza: report via Creator Info, then Report, then Report Offensive Content. Flagged content goes to a human moderator queue, and report volume does not change its priority. Penalties range from warnings up to permanent and hardware bans for zero-tolerance content. — [Forza Support: Report UGC](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/4409070620819-How-to-Block-Mute-or-Report-a-Player-or-Report-Inappropriate-UGC-in-Forza-Horizon); [Forza Code of Conduct](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/360035563914-Forza-Code-of-Conduct)
- FH6 (Oct–Nov 2026): backlash over anime/itasha livery bans. Playground clarified that itasha is allowed but sexually suggestive content is not (cited cues include "languid gaze" and "seductive clothing"). Reported figures: 21,900 reports (about 0.1% of content), 3% led to penalties, under 700 users affected. These numbers are company-reported via press and unverified. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/forza-horizon-6-livery-moderation-major-community-backlash/); [ixbt.games](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/11/01/razrabotciki-forza-horizon-6-obieiasnili-za-cto-baniat-anime-skiny-tomnyi-vzgliad-i-soblaznitelnaia-odezda-pod-zapretom.html); [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/forza-horizon-6-itasha-allowed-human-moderators-review-reports.445957/)
- GT7 limits uploads to small SVGs (15 KB) through a web uploader tied to the PSN account. — [RacingGames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/article/how-to-upload-decals-to-gran-turismo-7)

### Inferences
- Recommended stack for derby: keep paint in a per-car UV-space render texture (or a layer list baked to a texture) rather than world-projected decals, so paint follows mesh deformation. Then drive a per-vertex (or low-res texture) damage mask that reveals clear coat dulling, primer, bare metal and rust, plus a separate dirt/mud mask that accumulates over the match. Players' paint should visibly get destroyed, which is the point of the genre.
- Store liveries as a compact layer list (shapes, text, number, sticker IDs, transforms, colors) plus optional freehand strokes. Rebuild the texture on clients for cheap netcode and replays.
- Moderation in layers: text slots (car name, slogan, sponsor) go through a profanity filter; image upload is restricted or omitted (vector-only or curated stickers lower the risk); public sharing gets report-to-human-queue; and an option shows only friends' or default liveries in public lobbies. Clear published rules avoid Forza-style backlash. Mirroring the real "no offensive words/pictures" derby rule fits the theme.

- No sourced triplanar-paint approach for cars was found.
- No information on Wreckfest's (original) livery editor internals or sharing.
