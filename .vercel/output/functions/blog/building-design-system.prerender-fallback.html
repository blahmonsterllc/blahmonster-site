Building a Design System

📅February 19, 2024

A design system is more than a component library—it's a shared language between design and development.

Start small. Document patterns as you go. Iterate based on real usage. Most importantly, make it easy to adopt.

The best design systems grow organically from actual product needs, not theoretical perfection.
